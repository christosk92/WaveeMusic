**"Add a music folder" from a playlist's greyed local files.** When a playlist lists local files that are not in your
music folders, the "can't be played" bar now says so ("…a local file that isn't in your music folders") and offers
"Add a music folder" beside "Hide it", which opens Settings › Storage › Music on this PC. The bar's buttons now slide
instead of jumping when its count changes ("Hide them" to "Hide it"). In Classic, WinUI and Zune. (#240)
