**Your own video for a song is now one of its versions.** Dropping an .mp4 on a track, or Video › Link a video file…
in its menu, adds the file to your library and links it to the track. It is no longer a separate "attachment". The
track shows its video mark and offers its video in the player and Now playing, as before. Its Versions and formats list
shows the file as "Your video · 1280×720". "Play by default" can pick the video, and the song then plays as that video
wherever you play it. Videos you attached in an earlier version move over by themselves on first start, missing files
included. Settings › Playback › Linked videos lists them, with Replace, Locate, Show in Explorer and Unlink. Unlinking
keeps the file in your library. Track details no longer shows a placeholder that never fills for a song whose only
video is yours. (#240)
