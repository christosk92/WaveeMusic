**Your other devices see what Wavee plays from Local files.** While Wavee plays its Local files list, the Spotify app
on your phone or another computer now shows it the way it shows its own: "Local Files" as the source, the song and
the ones before and after it named by their artist, album and title (never a folder or a path), the file's real length,
and no offer to move playback to a device that cannot have the file. A play of a local song is kept in your
listening history, and a local song's playback is reported the way the Spotify app reports one. When another device
asks Wavee to play *its* Local Files, Wavee now leaves what it is playing alone instead of claiming to play Local
Files while it plays something else. (#240)
