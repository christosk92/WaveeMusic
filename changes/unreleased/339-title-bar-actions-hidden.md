**The title bar's buttons no longer go missing until you resize.** In Classic, bell, friend activity, pin and full screen
sometimes stayed hidden after Wavee started (or a tab closed) in a window that had room for them, and came back only once
the window was resized. They now appear as soon as they fit; resizing still keeps them from flickering at the edge.
