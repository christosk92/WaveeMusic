**Your files and Spotify: covers and correct names for the music on your PC, shown in Wavee only.** On this PC offers it once
("Wavee can add covers and correct names from Spotify to 42 albums"); turning matching on looks your albums up on Spotify in the
background, album first, using only titles, artists and ISRCs from your tags, never paths or files. The library chip counts
("Matching · 12 of 48") and one toast says what was found. Review matches lists every album and song with its confidence (Exact,
Strong, Likely, Check) and why, in plain words ("Same ISRC on 11 of 11 songs", "10 of 10 track lengths line up"): Accept, see the
field-by-field Details and choose whose info wins, pick another candidate, search Spotify yourself, or mark it Not on Spotify, which
sticks. Nothing changes until you accept, and Exact matches are accepted for you only if you tick that at the opt-in. An accepted
album shows Spotify's names and covers in Wavee while your files stay untouched, its songs' Versions and formats offer the Spotify
version beside your file, and Undo or Remove link puts your own info back at once. A song's row menu has "Spotify match…", and
Settings › Storage › Music on this PC holds the switch. Untagged files named "Artist - Title" now show that title and artist. WinUI
and Classic draw the review as settings cards with an InfoBar; Zune in its own voice, lowercase with text verbs. (#240, Your files
and Spotify, phase 1.)
