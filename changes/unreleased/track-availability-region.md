**Songs that aren't available in your country are greyed out, and say so.** A song Spotify won't play where you are (no
audio for your country and no replacement version) used to look playable and then fail with "This track isn't available".
It's now greyed out the way the Spotify app shows it, and the reason leads its second line: "Not available in your country",
"Premium only", the release date for a song that isn't out yet, or "Unavailable". The track details say it too. The bar
above the list counts these songs by reason, and "Hide them" hides them. Playing one shows a notice with the reason, and
the queue skips it. A song Spotify replaces with another version that plays in your country still plays normally. In all three themes.
