**On this PC's Home is useful with songs that have no album.** A library of loose songs (files without an album tag) saw
only "Every shelf on Home is hidden", though no shelf was hidden: the shelves held albums only. Now Recently added shows
those songs as cards, grouped by when they arrived ("1 song this month"), Lossless includes lossless songs, and Shuffle
by decade counts songs that carry a year; a shelf with nothing to show is simply left out. "Every shelf on Home is
hidden" shows only when you hid them all in Edit home. Music folders with no song Wavee can play show "No songs here
yet" with Add music, instead of an empty page. In Classic, WinUI and Zune. (#240)
