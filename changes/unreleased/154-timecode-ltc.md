**Send timecode to lighting and video software.** Settings › Sound › Timecode plays an SMPTE linear timecode (LTC) signal on a
second output while your music plays as usual, so a lighting desk, Resolume or TouchDesigner can follow the song: 24, 25,
29.97 drop-frame or 30 fps, every track starting at 00:00:00:00 or 01:00:00:00, with a level and an offset dial. The timecode
follows seeks, pauses and track changes and goes silent while nothing plays. It never plays on the output your music uses.
In all three themes.
