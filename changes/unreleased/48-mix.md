**Spotify's Mix plays in Wavee.** A playlist Spotify has mixed now plays with its transitions: a pair edited in Spotify
(Fade or Rise) plays at its saved timing, and every other pair gets Wavee's own Auto transition, a beat-aligned Fade
that is shorter when the tempos are far apart. The next song can start mid-file on a downbeat, the bass hands over and
the filter sweeps as the preset describes, and the song on screen changes when the outgoing one is over. Shuffled pairs
get Auto too. A local file, a song you queued, an episode or a video still gets your normal Crossfade or gapless join.
Spotify-compatible: the curves are Spotify's, the sound is Wavee's own mixer, so a saved transition sounds close to,
not identical with, the official app.
