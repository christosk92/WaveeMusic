**Date added shows whenever the song list has room for it.** A playlist or Liked Songs no longer hides its Date added
column in a mid-size window or beside a docked queue while the title column has plenty of space (WinUI, and Classic and
Zune in a small window). In WinUI, where the Album column does not fit, the album moves next to the artist under the song
title instead of disappearing.
