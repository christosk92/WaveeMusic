**Files on this PC play here while your phone is playing.** With Spotify playing on another device (an iPhone, a speaker),
dropping a song or video on Wavee, double-clicking a row on "On this PC" or pressing Play all sent the play to that device,
which cannot open a file on this computer, so nothing played. A file on this PC now always plays on this PC: playback
moves here and the other device stops, as in Spotify's own app. Play all on "On this PC" also works again: it played
nothing once the list had loaded. (All three themes.) (#240)
