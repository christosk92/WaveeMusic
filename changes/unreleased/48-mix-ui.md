**See and switch Mix.** A playlist Spotify has mixed shows a lit Mix button in its list bar and "Mixed · 2 of 9
transitions edited" on its meta line. The button turns Mix on for a playlist of your own, or lets this PC listen without
it. While a transition plays, the player bar's time reads "Mixing into <next song>". A song's menu in a mixed playlist
offers "Don't mix into the next song", which makes that one pair a normal track change on this PC. All three themes.

**Transitions, quietly.** In a mixed playlist the gap under a row shows a thin line and "⋯ Transition" when you point at it, an
edited pair keeps a small dot and a "No mix" pair a break. "Show transitions: Always" in the new Mix menu puts a label in every gap
instead ("Auto", "Rise · 2.9 s", "No mix"). The Transitions list (the Mix menu, or a click on a gap) lists every pair with its
style, length and whether it is Auto, edited or set on this PC. The Mix menu also offers "Listen without Mix" and "Mix when
shuffling".
