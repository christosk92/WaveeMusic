**The queue shows the mix.** While a mixed playlist plays, the queue says how the next song comes in ("Mixing · Rise ·
2.9 s") between the playing song and the next one, and the Crossfade switch says "Mix is on for this playlist" where its
length usually sits. A mixed playlist's cover carries a small "Mixed" badge.
