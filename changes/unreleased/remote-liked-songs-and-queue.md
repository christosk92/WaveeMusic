**Liked Songs on your phone is Liked Songs in Wavee.** When your phone or another computer plays your Liked Songs, Wavee
names it Liked Songs, opening it goes to Wavee's own Liked Songs page, and that page's play button pauses and resumes it.
Your other devices also see Wavee's own Liked Songs playback as Liked Songs. While another device plays a playlist you
started from Wavee, sorting or filtering that playlist in Wavee changes the order there without restarting the song, and
"Play next" keeps that device's own upcoming and autoplay songs as they were.
