**Drop songs on Wavee and choose "Add to library".** When you drag songs or videos from Explorer onto Wavee, the drop panel
now offers a fourth choice beside Play now, Play next and Add to queue: Add to library keeps the files in Local files
without playing or queueing them, and says "Added 1 song to Local files". In Zune the four verbs keep one width when a
narrow window wraps the last one onto its own row. All themes.
