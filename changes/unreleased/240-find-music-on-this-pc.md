**Find music on this PC.** With no music folder yet, Local files, On this PC and Settings › Storage › Music folders now show
your Music and Downloads folders with how many songs each holds ("1,204 songs (238 in OneDrive)", "37 songs · 12 videos"),
counted from the folder listing without opening a file. Music is ticked, Downloads is not; a folder with no songs is greyed.
"Add selected" adds the ticked folders; nothing is added until you press it. "Choose another folder…" picks any other.
WinUI and Classic show it as a card, Zune in its own words. (#240)
