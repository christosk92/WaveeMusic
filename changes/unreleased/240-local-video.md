**Your own video files play as videos.** A music video you drop on Wavee or add to Local files (MP4, M4V, and MKV or WebM where
Windows has the codec) now plays with its picture, not as sound only: Now playing's cover turns into the video at the video's own
shape (16:9 for a music video), with the title and artist still under it, and full screen, the mini player and Switch to audio work
as they do for music videos on Spotify. The picture and sound stay together through seeks. Songs after a video play as before, and a
video never crossfades. Every place that shows the song's cover (the rows, the player bar, Up next, the Windows media card) shows a
still from the video, taken once and kept on disk. A video's own title and artist tags are used when it has them. In all three themes.
(#240, local videos.)
