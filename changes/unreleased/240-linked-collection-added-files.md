**A song you linked to Spotify, and a file you dropped in, are full members of your library.** A linked song now sits on
its Spotify album in Albums, with Spotify's cover. Its artist card in Artists shows the artist's Spotify picture instead
of the music-note placeholder. Before, an untagged file linked to "Savin' Me" showed "No albums yet". A file you drop
into Local Files from anywhere on your PC, music video included, now stays in your library after a restart. It shows on
Home and in the song count, and has the same menu as a music-folder file, including "Find on Spotify…".
"Remove from Local Files" takes it out again, with Undo. Every local row with a file behind it offers "Find on
Spotify…". Video files in your music folders are now listed and counted too. (#240)
