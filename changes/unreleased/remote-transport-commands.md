**Previous, shuffle and repeat on your other device work the way Spotify's own app sends them.** While a phone or
another computer is playing, Previous lets that device decide whether to restart the song or go back, as its own button
does, and each shuffle or repeat change reaches it as one command instead of two. Pause, resume and seek are sent in
the same form as Spotify's app too.
