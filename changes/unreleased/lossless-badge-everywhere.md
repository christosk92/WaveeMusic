**Lossless badge in every theme.** The "· Lossless" after the artists in the player bar now shows in WinUI and Zune
too (Zune's in its lowercase), with the exact format, such as FLAC 24/96, when you hover it. Settings > Sound >
Quality has a "Show the Lossless badge" switch to hide it, and when the badge appears a small tip above the player
bar asks whether to hide it or keep it.
