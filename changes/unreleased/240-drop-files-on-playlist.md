**Drop songs from your PC onto one of your playlists.** Dragging music files (or a folder of them) from File Explorer onto a
playlist in the sidebar now adds them to it, each as its own local-file row, the same way "Add to playlist" on a local song does,
and says "Added to <playlist name>". A file Wavee has not seen yet joins Local files first. WinUI and Classic (Zune has no sidebar playlists). (#240)
