**Local files you can't play can be dealt with.** A song in Local files that Wavee can't play now says why on its own
line ("Can't play: a WAV file", "Missing", "Offline") instead of a clipped "Unavailab" in the time column, which keeps
the song's length. Right-clicking a local song no longer offers what only a Spotify song can do (Save, Share, song radio,
Video); it adds Show in folder and a way to take the song out: "Hide from Local Files" for a song in a music folder
(the file stays on your PC and is left out of future scans) or "Remove from Local Files" for a dropped file, each with
Undo. Local files no longer show a heart. "See which" under "Needs attention" now opens the Review dialog, which lists
the files Wavee can't play next to the missing ones, each with Show in folder and Hide, so the attention count can
always be cleared. (#240)
