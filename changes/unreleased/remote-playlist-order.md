**A playlist started on your other device plays in the order you see in Wavee.** While a phone or another computer is
playing, starting a playlist from Wavee sends its songs in the order and with the filter you have on the page, and starts
the very row you clicked, also when a song is in the list twice. An album starts at the song you clicked, and an artist's
Popular songs play as that artist's Popular list, the way Spotify's own app starts them.
