**Ctrl+Left / Ctrl+Right skip tracks while a list row has keyboard focus.** After tabbing into a playlist or any song list, the
arrow keys a list can't use (Left and Right on a list that scrolls up and down) now reach the app, so Ctrl+Left and Ctrl+Right
skip to the previous and next song there too. Up and Down still move through the rows, and grids keep all four arrows.
