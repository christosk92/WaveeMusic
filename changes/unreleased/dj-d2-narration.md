**The DJ talks.** When DJ plays on Wavee, its voice now comes between the songs as Spotify's own app plays it: a welcome
when it starts, an intro before each run of songs and a short sign-off after it, each played in turn with the music
starting as the voice ends. While the DJ talks, the player shows "Welcome" or "Up next" from "DJ X" with its picture and no
times; seeking, repeat-one and moving playback to another device wait until it is done, Next goes straight to the music
and Previous plays the song it belongs to from the start. The voice never shows in the queue or in what you played. A
line that cannot be fetched is skipped and the music goes on. (All three themes.)
