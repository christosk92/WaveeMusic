**DJ works across your devices.** While Wavee plays Spotify's DJ, it now tells your other devices what the official
desktop app tells them: that DJ is playing, which segment the song is from, and that shuffle and repeat are off for DJ,
so the phone's Now Playing and controls match; while the DJ talks, they show it talking, as with the desktop app. Starting DJ in Wavee while your phone or another speaker is playing now
starts DJ there, as the desktop app does; Wavee used to name DJ as an ordinary playlist, which has no songs of its own.
When another device plays DJ, Wavee's shuffle and repeat buttons show it as DJ does ("Not available with DJ"), and while
the DJ talks Wavee no longer offers a seek or a move to another speaker that the DJ would refuse.
Moving DJ from your phone to Wavee, or restarting Wavee while DJ was playing, keeps the song you were on (while the DJ was
talking: the song it was talking about, from its start) and carries on with DJ's next songs, with shuffle and repeat off as
before; the DJ does not welcome you a second time.
