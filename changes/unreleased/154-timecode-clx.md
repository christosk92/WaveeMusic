**Send what's playing as CLX on your network.** Settings › Sound › Timecode has a "Send CLX on the network" switch. With it
on, Wavee sends the position, title and artist of what plays to the CLX multicast group, so Clockworks-compatible software on
the same network (Timecode Toolbox and others) can follow the song without a cable. It follows seeks, pauses and track changes.
