**Spotify Connect shows the real audio quality, both ways.** While Wavee plays lossless, your phone's Spotify app now shows it
as Lossless instead of High: Wavee tells Connect what it actually plays (Lossless, Lossless 24-bit, Very high, High or Normal),
and says so again when the quality changes mid-session, for example when a lossless key is refused and Wavee falls back to Very
high. While another device plays, the device flyout says what that device plays ("Lossless · set by the phone") and where (its
headphones or speaker, as it names them), and the player bar shows the Lossless badge when it plays lossless. In all three themes.
