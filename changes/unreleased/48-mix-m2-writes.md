**Turn off Mix for everyone.** On a playlist of your own, or one you collaborate on, "Turn off Mix for everyone" in the Mix
menu now turns Mix off on Spotify, so every listener and every device plays the playlist unmixed. Your transitions are kept:
the note that confirms it offers "Turn on again", and turning Mix back on brings every edited transition back. All three
themes.

**Edited transitions are saved to Spotify.** On a playlist you can edit, Save in a transition's editor saves it to your
Spotify account, so the official app and your other devices play the same transition; "No mix" saves Spotify's "No
transition", and Reset to auto puts the pair back on Auto there too. The editor says "Saving to Spotify…" while it saves. When
you're offline, or Spotify doesn't take the change, the transition still plays your way on this PC, the editor says it isn't
saved yet and offers Retry, and the pair's chip in the list says "Not saved". On a playlist you can't edit, edits stay on
this PC as before ("Saved on this PC only"). The song menu's "Don't mix into the next song" saves the same way. All three
themes.

**"Listen without Mix" follows your account.** Choosing it in the Mix menu now tells Spotify, so your other devices play
that playlist unmixed for you too, and choosing it again undoes it everywhere. Playlists you set to listen without Mix on
this PC before are sent to your account once, the first time Wavee connects. When Spotify can't take the choice right
away, it applies here at once and reaches your account the next time Wavee connects.

**Mixed playlists open with their transitions at once.** Wavee keeps a mixed playlist's transitions with the playlist on
this PC, so after a restart its seams, edited transitions and Mix menu are there before Spotify answers; when the
playlist page opens, Wavee asks Spotify whether any transition changed elsewhere and reads the playlist again only then.
