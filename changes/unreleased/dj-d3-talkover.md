**The DJ talks over the music, and its songs run into each other.** The DJ's voice no longer waits in silence for the
songs: an intro talks over the start of the song it introduces, which comes in quietly under the voice and swells up as it
finishes (timed to the song's own intro point when Spotify marks one), a sign-off talks over the last few seconds of a run
with the song turned down beneath it, and after "Let DJ pick" the new song starts under the DJ's voice. Between the DJ's own
songs, each one fades out on its end point while the next fades in on its start point, as Spotify's DJ mixes them, instead
of stopping and starting. Once the song is playing under the voice you can repeat it or move playback to another device;
seeking waits until the voice is done, and Next skips both the voice and the song under it. Other devices see the same fades
and the same DJ state Spotify's own app shows.
