**Home no longer jumps when it finishes loading.** While Home loaded, the daylist and every Spotify section stood as a
thin line, then grew to full size as they arrived and pushed your shortcuts and the rest of the page down. Each one now
waits as a placeholder in its own shape and size (the daylist card, the shelves), so the page fills in where it stands.
