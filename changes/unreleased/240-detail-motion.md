**Opening a folder or an album on On this PC moves like opening a page.** A folder (Folders) or an album (Albums, and
Home's shelves) now opens with a header that shows its cover, name and counts, and the cover flies from the tile you
clicked into that header (WinUI) while the rest fades in. Back, the breadcrumb or Esc reverses it: the cover flies back
into its tile and the list you came from is where you left it. An album opened from Albums or Home goes back to Albums or
Home, not to the Folders tab. The title bar's Back button now steps out of an open folder before it leaves the page.
Classic and Zune open and close the same way with a quick fade. (#240)
