**Friend activity shows your friends.** The friend activity panel always said "Sign in to see what your friends are
playing", even while signed in. It now lists what the people you follow are playing, the ones listening right now first
and then the most recent, each with when they started, and the list follows along as
they change songs. An account that follows nobody sees the empty panel, and a failed
load keeps the rows it had or offers Retry. In every theme.
