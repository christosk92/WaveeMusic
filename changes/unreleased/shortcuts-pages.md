**Your shortcuts keep their shape when the window changes.** After the side panel opened or the window was resized, the
shortcuts grid on Home kept the width it had before: tiles in one row came out different sizes, the last tile ran past
the edge, and turning the page showed a cut-off piece of the page before it and a tile with no picture or name. In every
theme, each page is now exactly as wide as the grid and the tiles follow a new width at once. In WinUI and Zune, the
arrow that appears on hover to go to the next page now sits on the right edge, not the left.
