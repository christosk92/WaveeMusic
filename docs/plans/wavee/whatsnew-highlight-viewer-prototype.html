Why the card fill is opaque

A gradient can only end in a colour that is really there. Interaction.Card paints white @ 5 % over the plate and cross-fades to 3 % on hover — no end-stop matches both. So this one card carries Tok.FillSolidTertiary and a flat hover: the stroke lifts, the fill does not.

The fade is measured, not guessed

The paragraph lays out unclamped inside the clipped slot; a bounds watcher sets overflows = h > 68.5. Line height is an explicit 17, so natural heights are 51 / 68 / 85 — never within half a pixel of the threshold. A body that fits draws no fade (card 3 at the page width shows it).

The viewer

  • W = clamp(320, min(960, vpW−96, (vpH−360)×16⁄9)) — — at this window size
  • Image above text: every asset is 16:9 and the bodies are long; side-by-side needs ≥ 1100 DIP
  • Clamped, no wrap — at an end the arrow dims in place, never vanishes; dots say where you are
  • 250 ms direction-aware slide, ±24 DIP; instant under reduced motion

Try it

  • Click a card, or Tab to one and press Enter
  • ← → step · Home End jump · Esc or the scrim closes
  • The store card has two targets: the hit region opens the viewer, the button opens the Store
