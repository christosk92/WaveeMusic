SidebarSectionKind.CollectionShortcuts — the section carrying Liked Songs, Albums, Artists, Podcasts and Local files with count badges — is absent from LibraryV3Document.Build entirely. The counts are already warm (EnsureStats() is called by LibraryV3Sidebar) and the routes are already registered (ShellRoutes.cs:31-35) — nothing renders them.
