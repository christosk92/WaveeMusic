Daylist hero: artist-style fade

The daylist header art fills the whole hero and a veil fades it under the text, the way the artist page does. Pick A (keep the card) or B (borderless, bleeds into the page). Both use the two-line countdown and full bars for finished dayparts.

What each variant is in code
  • A · Card — the Ui.Card stays (border, 8 px corners, 32/12/12/12 padding). The art is Controls.CoverFill across the whole card, clipped to its corners, with Palette.ArtistHeroVeil (horizontal) over it; the text column is about half the card, never under 340.
  • B · Bleed — no card frame. The art spans the Home content width at the page gutter and fades out at the bottom (EdgeFade Bottom, the artist hero's 28 % band); the same veil from the left.
  • Both: the countdown is two lines (the count, then "wednesday evening arrives at 16:35", named from the next timeline segment), and finished dayparts fill their bars.
