The Collection browser

One browser, two pivots, one album pane. Albums keeps its master-detail split; Artists loses the empty tile grid and its third pane for a Zune-style reader: the artist's albums stacked as art + track blocks with a cover spine to jump between them. Sort is a word rail, alphabetical lists get letter groups and an A–Z strip, and every loading state is a counted shimmer, never a blank tile. Everything below is live: click artists, albums, sort words, spine covers, tracks. Data is fake and marked as such.

What changed, and why

  • One album pane, one look. 0.2.10 already had one pane mounted twice, but width tiering and the notice bar made it look like two different pages. 0.3's Album.Pane has one anatomy at every width, demands the whole album on mount and re-projects when the store changes. The "minified view" notice and the Plays column are gone: plays, credits and recommendations live on the full page, which the title and Open album link to.
  • Artists: reader, not grid. The middle grid of 140-px cards was three panes fighting for 1140 px, and it painted blank tiles when discography rows arrived unhydrated. The reader stacks the artist's albums as art + tracks blocks in a single scroll, the Zune "album view inside an artist" model. A 36-px cover spine at the left is the jump list; the current block highlights as you scroll.
  • Library first, catalogue second. The reader opens on in your library (saved albums, all hydrated from the library snapshot). All releases appends the rest of the discography in batches with counted skeleton blocks, so there is never a tile that has nothing to say.
  • Word rails instead of dropdowns. Sort is Zune's text pivot: active word at 100 %, the others at 50 %, an accent underline. It fits a 280-px column, needs no flyout, and reads as the same control everywhere.
  • Letters. Alphabetical sort gets sticky letter groups and an A–Z strip on the column's edge (Zune's type-ahead jump, made visible). Letters with no rows are dimmed.
  • Rows say something. Artist rows read "3 albums · 34 songs" rather than the literal word "Artist"; album rows keep artist + year.

Zune: taken, adapted, left

  • Taken — text as navigation (word rails), opacity-encoded state (100 / 85 / 50 %), the collection filtering left to right, the "all" row (in your library / all releases), album view inside the artist, type-ahead letter jump, content before chrome (one hairline between rungs, no card borders, no shadows on rows).
  • Adapted — Zune's edge-bleeding lowercase Display type becomes Fluent's 28/36/600 PageHero; the faded artist canvas becomes an optional low-opacity art wash (toggle above). It breaks chapter 15 §0.8 ("accent-neutral surface") only if the CTA takes the cover colour, which it does not.
  • Left — the three-column artists ▸ albums ▸ songs browser at desktop widths (it is what produced the squeezed grid), the mixview constellation, zero-radius everything (the app is Fluent, radius 5/8 stays).

Chapter 15 amendments, if approved

  • §0.2 keeps the hairline rungs; §0.5's "four view types × three sizes" shrinks to list / grid × S/M/L for the navigator, and the discography column's second copy of the control is deleted with the column.
  • W2 is replaced by the reader (2 panes). W8's discography skeleton becomes counted skeleton blocks. W9's three placeholders become two.
  • §9 "Album.Page.cs must carry two components": confirmed — Album.Page (owner M) and Album.Pane (owner M, first deliverable of Wave 5, consumed by O and N). The Artists reader is Artist.Reader in Artist.UI.cs (owner N) and reuses the block from Album.Pane.
  • §7 data: the pane demands the whole album model (tracks, durations, hearts); the reader demands saved albums from the library edges, then the discography in 300-id batches per the query-layer rule. No page-side windows.
  • Persisted per kind: column width, sort word, view, size, scope word (library/all), selection. Filter text still not persisted.
