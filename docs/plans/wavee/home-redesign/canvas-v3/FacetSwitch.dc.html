Facet switch: motion & states

A facet is a different page from the server, so a switch is a page load, and it never shows a skeleton or a shimmer. The old page stays in place, dims to 60% and stops taking input, and a 3px indeterminate ProgressBar pins to the bottom edge of the facet control. When the data lands the old page fades out in 83ms and the new one enters (250ms, 8px rise, 30ms per zone). Following behaves the same way, because it is a facet too. Frames are drawn at 100% (1440 wide), cropped to the top of the window.
