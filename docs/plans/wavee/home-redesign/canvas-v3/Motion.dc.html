Scroll & motion
Home scrolls as a set of chapters, not one long poster. The daylist card and Recently played settle in once. After that, each chapter's header pins under the compact facet band until the next chapter pushes it away, and shelves turn in whole pages. Motion uses the stock WinUI durations (83 / 167 / 250 / 333 ms) and the Fast-Out-Slow-In curve cubic-bezier(0,0,0,1). Nothing bounces, glows or lifts. The facet row and its switch motion have their own board (Facet switch). The title bar, icon rail and now-playing bar are fixed chrome and take no part in this choreography.

At rest: top of Home
The daylist card fades and rises 8px once (333ms). Recently played items follow, 30ms apart. Nothing lower on the page animates on load. The countdown starts ticking immediately.

"Made for you" reaches the top of the layer and pins at top:44, just under the compact facet band, with its pager still in reach. It's opaque with a Mica-blurred backdrop, so cards slide under it cleanly.
3
Chapter hand-off
The next header ("It's New Music Friday!") pushes the pinned one up and out: native position:sticky inside each chapter, scroll-linked and with no timed animation. Only one chapter header is ever pinned.
4
Shelf page turn
track moves by exactly one page, 1332px, 250ms
page 1: the wide lead = 2 cells, so page 2 starts on a card edge
Chevron or pip click: the track translates by one viewport plus one gap. Every template divides it evenly: 6 × 206, 3 × 428 and 8 × 150.5 all step 1332px, and a wide lead counts as two cells. No card is ever cut at rest.
Timing & easing
Daylist card entrance: 333ms, cubic-bezier(0,0,0,1), opacity 0→1 plus 8px rise, first load only
Recently played stagger: 250ms per item, 30ms step, 8 items
Pointer-over fill (buttons, rows, item backplates): 83ms linear, fill only
Hover play button on a cover: 167ms fade plus 4px rise
Lead / 16:9 image hover: 2% zoom inside a fixed frame, 333ms
Shelf page turn: 250ms, cubic-bezier(0,0,0,1), transform only
Pressed: a lower fill and dimmed text. Never scale or lift.
Sticky stack
Title bar: fixed Wavee chrome, 48px
Icon rail: fixed chrome, 56px wide
Facet row (All / Music / Podcasts / Audiobooks): not sticky; it scrolls away with the page. Past scrollTop 64 the compact band (Segmented control + Following) takes over: sticky top:0, 44px, z 45
Chapter headers (Made for you, It's New Music Friday!, Because you like…, Jump back in, Radio & mixes, Browse): sticky top:44px, 52px, z 20, opaque with a Mica blur, bleeding to the layer edges
Not sticky: the daylist card and Recently played (the page's top matter)
Now-playing bar: fixed chrome at the bottom
Paging & keyboard
Viewport overflow:hidden with an 8px bleed so hover backplates aren't clipped. The track uses translateX(-page × 1332px).
Chevrons disable at the ends, and pips are buttons that jump straight to a page.
Keyboard: Tab moves into a shelf, the arrow keys move between items, and moving past the last visible item turns the page. The focus rectangle is always the two-tone Windows one.
Not paged by design: Recently played (fixed 4×2), Because you like… (grouped cards), Browse (a grid of tiles).
