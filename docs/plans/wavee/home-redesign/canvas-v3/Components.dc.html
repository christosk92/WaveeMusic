Component sheet
The reference for pixel values. Every Home control is a stock WinUI 3 control or a GridView/ListView item template: 4px radius for in-page elements and cover art, 8px only for cards, 32px buttons, the Windows type ramp (12 / 14 / 20 / 28 / 40, Regular and Semibold, nothing under 12px), and the two-tone focus rectangle. Nothing lifts on hover. Hover changes the fill, press lowers it and dims the text. If Main or Light ever disagrees with a value here, this sheet wins.
1 · Buttons
Accent is only for the single primary action (Play). Standard is for secondary actions (Shuffle, genre picks). Subtle is for icon-only actions (like, more, row play). HyperlinkButton is for "See all". All are 32px tall except hyperlink/pager (28px), 4px radius, 14px text, with a 1px elevation stroke (the top edge is lighter). There are no pills.

12 · Facet word (page-title pivot)
The facet row is the page's heading, so it's type, not tabs: Title 28/36 in Segoe UI Variable Display, Regular + TextSecondary when not selected and Semibold + TextPrimary when selected. Selection is weight and colour, never colour alone. There's no underline, no fill at rest and no divider. Each word is a 40px box with 8px side padding and a 4px radius, and the boxes sit 24px apart (about 40px glyph to glyph). The first box is pulled 8px left so its glyphs align with the content edge. A word is disabled only when offline and its facet has no cached page (“Not available offline”). Arrow cursor, never the hand.

15 · ProgressBar (a facet switch in flight)
An indeterminate WinUI ProgressBar: two accent indicators sweep over a 1px track on a 2s loop. It's 3px tall and runs the full content width, pinned to the bottom edge of whichever facet control is showing. Under the band, its track replaces the divider. While it runs, the old page stays at 60% and ignores input. It is never a spinner in the middle of the page and never a skeleton. Accessible name: “Loading {facet}”.

16 · Episode row
A ListView row for episodes: 76px tall with 56px cover art at 4px radius, a Body Strong title clamped to 2 lines and a 12px caption: show · date · duration. A 12px video glyph (TextTertiary) sits before the duration of a video episode. The E badge (16px, 1px stroke) sits before the show name of an explicit one. An in-progress episode gets a 2px hairline under the caption, showing the real fraction with a 2px minimum, and a caption of “44 min left” in place of the date. On hover the row gets a 4px backplate and a 32px round accent Play appears over the art.
