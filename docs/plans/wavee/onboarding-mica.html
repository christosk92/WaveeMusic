Before we start

Terms & what Wavee is

Wavee is an independent desktop client for your own Spotify account. It is not made by, endorsed by, or affiliated with Spotify AB.

What Wavee needs from you
Your Spotify Premium accountWavee streams full-quality, on-demand audio, which Spotify only allows on Premium accounts. Free accounts can browse, but playback is blocked.
A one-time local playback componentDownloaded from Spotify's own CDN, verified by fingerprint and signature, and stored per-machine. Nothing else is installed and nothing is bundled with Wavee.
Local data on this PCLogin, library, metadata cache, settings and logs live under %LOCALAPPDATA%\Wavee. Factory reset erases all of it.
Trademarks

Spotify® and the Spotify logo are registered trademarks of Spotify AB. All trademarks, logos and brand names are the property of their respective owners; their use here is nominative only. Wavee itself ships under its own licence — the full text is in Settings → About → Licences.

Read the full agreement 4 sections
1 · Base software & copyrightWavee's own source is licensed to you under the terms shipped in the About tab. You may not redistribute a build that removes the attribution above.
2 · Your account & your librarySigning in lets Wavee read and modify your Spotify library — saves, follows, playlist edits, playback state — on your behalf, using Spotify's own APIs.
3 · Streaming & online servicesUsing Spotify through Wavee means Spotify's own terms of service still apply to your account and your listening.
4 · Local files & cachesEncrypted audio bodies and licence keys are cached locally to avoid re-downloading. You control the budget and the location, and can clear both at any time.
