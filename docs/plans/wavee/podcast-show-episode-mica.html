The podcast reader

Same two panes as every detail page. The left rail is the show's identity and never moves; the right column is a reader whose first screen answers the question the visitor actually has — "what is this and where do I start?" for a new show, "where was I and what's new?" for one you follow. An episode gets its own page with chapters, a readable transcript and comments. Flip visit and scene below; data shows what 0.3 can draw today versus after the fetches listed at the bottom.

The decisions

  • One frame, three visits. ShowVisit.Of(followed, anyProgress, newSinceLastPlay) → New · Returning · CaughtUp, derived on the model. The rail's accent pill follows it: Follow → Resume · 23 min left → Play latest.
  • New: doors, not a wall. Up to three "start here" cards — episode 1 leads for a serial, the latest leads for an episodic show, the trailer joins when one exists. The blurb moves right at full measure, with cadence and first-episode date as facts.
  • Returning: continue, then what's new. One hero with time left in the Zune clock voice, the old app's listen-next walk (now honouring consumption_order), then episodes published since your last play.
  • A reader, not boxed cards. Hairlines, month headers in Display Light, the episode number as a big light numeral. Played rows recede to 58 %; the playing row gets the tone bar and an equalizer.
  • Word rails, persisted per show. Filter and sort are words; the rail sticks while you scroll and survives the narrow layout (today it vanishes under 540).
  • The show's own colour. Wash, pills, progress and ticks take the cover's extracted tone — never brand green, the rule the WinUI app already learned.
  • Episode page = rail + reader. about · chapters · transcript · comments as a Display word rail; timestamps in the description seek; below it prev/next doors, more from the show, and the recommendations the old app fetched but never mounted.
