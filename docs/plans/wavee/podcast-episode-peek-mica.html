About

Focus is one of those things everyone swears they need more of and almost no one is actually addressing correctly. In this episode, we dig into what the science actually says about attention spans (hint: your brain isn’t broken, it’s just overwhelmed), why the standard advice to “try harder” is probably making things worse, and what’s really driving your inability to sit down and get things done. We get into the neuroscience of explore versus exploit modes, why flow states feel the way they do, and the four hidden triggers behind most focus problems — none of which have anything to do with your phone. Then we get into the practical stuff: environmental design, deep work frameworks, the maker versus manager schedule, time boxing, batching, and why a dentist appointment in the middle of your morning can ruin your entire day.

Rule. The rail clamps to four lines. Show more only appears when the text actually overflows, so a one-paragraph episode has no button and no empty affordance.
